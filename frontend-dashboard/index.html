4. 20-Streak Autonomy & High-Risk Guardrails: Routine technical queries unlock autonomous auto-sending once a category reaches 20 consecutive unmodified human approvals. However, Financial Refunds and Custom Quotes are hard-locked — human review remains permanently mandatory to prevent unauthorized fund transfers or legal liabilities.
